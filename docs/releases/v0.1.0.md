## Pinshot v0.1.0

Pinshot 首个正式版本，面向 GNOME Shell 51（Wayland）的轻量级截图、非窗口贴图、滚动长截图与 GIF 录制工具。

### 主要功能

- **区域与全屏截图**：冻结桌面并用八个手柄微调选区，支持复制、保存、1:1 原位贴图与标注；支持一键截取当前显示器全屏。
- **非窗口桌面贴图**：由 GNOME Shell 直接绘制，置顶且跟随工作区，不进入 `Alt+Tab`；支持拖拽、滚轮缩放、`Ctrl`+滚轮调不透明度、迷你缩略图态、点击穿透及历史恢复。
- **剪贴板贴图**：支持剪贴板图片、本地图片 URI 及纯文本/代码高分屏渲染卡片，保留原始显示倍率。
- **滚动长截图**：通过 ScreenCast Portal + PipeWire 纵向或横向采集拼接，自动处理固定栏与圆角边缘，支持两端补图。
- **GIF 录制**：开录前可选 5 / 10 / 16 / 24 / 30 FPS，录入鼠标指针，自动限尺寸与体积上限。
- **原生集成**：提供 GNOME 顶栏图标菜单（支持彩色/单色主题切换与临时禁用全局快捷键）、`.desktop` 应用入口与扩展首选项界面；`satty` 与 `libnotify` 为可选依赖。

### 安装

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dgxz99/pinshot/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
```

## English

First official release of Pinshot — a lightweight screenshot, non-window screen-pinning, scrolling capture, and GIF recording tool for GNOME Shell 51 on Wayland.

### Highlights

- **Region & fullscreen capture:** Freeze the desktop and adjust the selection with eight handles, or grab the active monitor fullscreen. Copy, save, pin 1:1 in place, or annotate.
- **Non-window desktop pins:** Drawn directly by GNOME Shell, staying above windows across workspaces without appearing in `Alt+Tab`. Supports dragging, wheel zoom, `Ctrl`+wheel opacity, mini mode, click-through, and history restore.
- **Clipboard pinning:** Pin clipboard images, local image URIs, or rendered text/code cards while preserving display scale.
- **Scrolling capture:** Vertical or horizontal stitching via ScreenCast Portal and PipeWire, preserving fixed headers/footers and extending either end.
- **GIF recording:** Choose 5 / 10 / 16 / 24 / 30 FPS before recording with pointer capture and automatic size/file-size bounds.
- **GNOME integration:** Native top-bar menu (color or monochrome symbolic icon, quick shortcut pause), `.desktop` launcher, and extension preferences. `satty` and `libnotify` are optional.
